How it works

  1. Pick a productType its name, or share a shop page with Franxit.
  2. Franxit analysesThe price in each shop, and what the product cost over the past year.
  3. You get adviceThe cheapest shop, and whether to buy now or wait.

What it looks like

Real screens from the app, with the real prices of 17 November 2026.

This is what each advice means:

  • Buy nowThe price is historically very attractive.
  • ConsiderGood, but not exceptional.
  • WaitNot particularly attractive. Wait, or set a price alert.
  • ExpensiveHigh compared with the price history.
  • No advice yetToo little history to judge. Franxit says so honestly.

What else you can do

  • History from day one

    No waiting for months: the prices of the past year are there straight away.

  • The price per shop

    You see what each shop asks, after their discounts, and tap through to the shop.

  • Your discount

    A discount through your employer? Enter it and see what you really pay.

  • Price alert and widget

    If the price drops below your amount, you get a notification. On your home screen you see the advice at a glance.

Why I made this

Before I buy something, I always try to find out two things: which shop is cheapest, and whether the price is good right now. That means going from shop to shop, and I still don't know whether that “-30%” is real.

Now Franxit does that for me. Maybe it will help you too.

How to get it on your phone

Franxit isn't on the Play Store, so Android asks for permission first.

  1. Tap the download button. Does your browser warn you? Choose Download anyway.
  2. Open franxit.apk. Android asks for permission. Tap Settings, switch it on and go back.
  3. Tap Install. Does Play Protect warn you? Choose More details, then Install anyway.

When a new version comes out, the app tells you. Install it over the old one. Your products stay.

Questions

How reliable is the advice?

As reliable as the price history. If a product has too little history, Franxit says "No advice yet" instead of guessing. It looks back and predicts nothing.

Does it cost anything?

No. No subscription and no ads. For extra shops through Google Shopping, you make a free account at SerpApi yourself.

Is it safe?

Franxit only asks for internet access and notifications. There's no account and your products stay on your phone. Franxit only counts, anonymously, how many devices use the app per day. The full story is here.

The warning when installing appears because the app isn't on the Play Store.

Where does the name come from?

From the Belgian franc and the English "sit". Sitting on your money until it's worth it.
